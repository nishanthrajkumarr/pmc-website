High-precision CNC machining

Our machining facility runs three vertical machining centres, two milling machines and an HMT lathe. We machine plates, brackets, frames and housings, 3D-contoured mould surfaces and turned parts for automotive, aerospace and heavy-machinery programmes.

  • Vertical machining on tables up to 1200 × 600 mm
  • 3D surface machining for moulds, dies and contoured parts
  • Turning on an HMT lathe with 1828 mm of Z-axis travel
  • Aluminium and steel parts, from one-off prototypes to repeat batches

Custom metal fabrication & assembly

Our fabrication shop turns sheet and structural steel into finished enclosures, frames and process equipment, with cutting, bending, welding and grinding done in-house.

  • Cutting, bending and forming of sheet metal
  • Welded frames, enclosures and structural assemblies
  • Blowers, ducting, cones and transitions
  • Finished assemblies, painted and fitted with motors and hardware

03 / 08

Castings: design, manufacture & assembly

We design and manufacture die-cast components and take them through machining and assembly, so you receive finished, ready-to-fit units instead of raw castings.

  • Die-cast aluminium housings, covers, elbows and fittings
  • Machining of cast parts to final dimensions
  • Assembly of valves, vents and multi-part units
  • Production runs supported by a dedicated assembly line
Quote this service See casting work
04 / 08

Component refurbishment & reverse engineering

When a part is worn, broken or no longer available, we measure the original, produce a new drawing and manufacture a replacement, or recondition the existing component so it can go back into service.

  • Measurement and redrawing of existing parts
  • Replacement parts for obsolete or unsupported equipment
  • Reconditioning and repair of worn components
  • Small batches to keep critical machines running
Quote this service
05 / 08

Mechanical power transmission & motion components

We make the hubs, housings and drive assemblies that move machines, and we build complete motion systems, from a clutch-driven power feed for a milling machine to drivetrains for robotics projects.

  • Hubs, flanges and housings for drive systems
  • Power feeds and clutch mechanisms for machine tools
  • Drivetrains and chassis assemblies for robots
  • Motion components built and assembled to your design

Tools, holders & vises

We supply tools, tool holders and vises, and machine custom workholding and fixtures that hold your parts accurately through every operation.

  • Tool holders and cutting tools for machining centres
  • Machine vises and workholding
  • Custom fixtures, jigs and clamping plates
  • Matched to your machines and part families

Grinding, surface finishing & treatments

Grinding, polishing and surface treatments take parts from machined to finished, whether that means a ground flat, a polished mould cavity, or an anodised or coated surface.

  • Surface grinding for flatness and finish
  • Polishing of mould cavities and visible surfaces
  • Anodising, coating and other surface treatments
  • Deburring and edge finishing before inspection
