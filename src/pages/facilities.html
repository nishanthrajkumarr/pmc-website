Our own floor. Our own standard.

Three facilities under one management: machining, fabrication, and die casting with assembly. Each is equipped and staffed to deliver consistent, inspected parts at production volume.

Machining Fabrication Die casting & assembly Machine list

01 Machining facility

Machining

Vertical machining centres from BFW and Cosmos, conventional milling machines and an HMT lathe, each chosen for its accuracy, reliability and ability to produce components that meet stringent industry standards.

  • 3 vertical machining centres (BFW, Cosmos)
  • 2 milling machines (Esteem, S&T)
  • HMT lathe with 1828 mm of Z-axis travel
  • Organised tool storage at every machine

Full machine list

02 Fabrication facility

Fabrication

A dedicated fabrication floor where skilled fabricators cut, bend, weld and grind sheet metal and structural steel into frames, enclosures and process equipment, in batches of every size.

  • Cutting and bending of sheet and plate
  • Welding and grinding stations
  • Batch production of welded frames and enclosures
  • Assembly with motors, fans and hardware

Fabrication work

03 Die casting & assembly facility

Die casting & assembly

Aluminium components are cast, machined and assembled on a structured line with dedicated workstations, then racked as finished stock ready for dispatch.

  • Die casting of aluminium components
  • Machining of castings to final size
  • Assembly line with dedicated workstations
  • Racked storage for finished parts and kits
