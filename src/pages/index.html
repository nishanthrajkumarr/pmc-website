01 About PMC

Twenty-five years of making parts to print.

PMC Industrial Solutions is a precision manufacturing firm led by partners P. Rajkumar and A. Saviour. We specialise in machining, forging, die casting and fabrication, and take customer drawings from the first prototype through to repeat production.

Our machining, fabrication and die-casting facilities are in India, and our office in Canada works with customers across North America. We quote and ship worldwide, and every order is quality-checked before it leaves the shop.

06 How it works

Drawing to delivery in five steps.

A straightforward process with one point of contact, whether you order one prototype or a production run.

  1. STEP 01

    Send your drawing

    Email a drawing or 3D model (PDF, DXF, STEP or IGES) with material, quantity and finish.

  2. STEP 02

    Review & quote

    We check manufacturability and reply with a price and lead time.

  3. STEP 03

    Manufacture

    Your parts are machined, cast or fabricated in our own facilities.

  4. STEP 04

    Inspect

    Every part is quality-checked against your drawing before packing.

  5. STEP 05

    Ship worldwide

    Packed for transit and shipped to your door, wherever you are.
